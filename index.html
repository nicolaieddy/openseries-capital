FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Can I invest today?

Not yet. The fund is in development and has not been registered with the SEC. Launch is targeted for 2027. Leave your details below and we will send the overview and keep you posted.

How can a fund value private companies every day?

The fund strikes its net asset value under a written valuation policy, checked against the index's daily marks and observed trades. The index itself uses Morningstar's published daily valuation method.

How do I get my money out?

Each quarter the fund offers to buy back between 5% and 25% of its shares at net asset value. If more investors ask to sell than the offer covers, requests are filled pro rata, so you may not be able to sell everything at once.

Does the fund hold every company in the index?

No. It holds a focused sample of the largest names, chosen to track the index while keeping trading costs down.

How do you avoid fake or layered SPV exposure?

Shares are bought directly from existing holders or from tightly vetted vehicles, and delivery is verified before settlement.

What will it cost?

A 1.25% management fee, with no performance fee. Platforms may add their own charges, and the fund's operating expenses will be set out in the prospectus.
